About The Space

Excellent opportunity to lease a 2.5 acre property with a 5,000 sq. ft. cinderblock building and a wood frame building. Possibly expandable by 6,500 sq. ft. Potentially total building area is 11,500 sq. ft. Great opportunity for a classic car or used car dealership. The building, constructed in 1982, is in good condition. Building is divided into two separate spaces: a three-bay shop space and a separate retail-display space. Immediate availability. Lease rate is $20.00/sq. ft. Net plus CAM and real estate taxes
